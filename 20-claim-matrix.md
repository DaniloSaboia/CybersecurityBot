**Evidence-basis warning:** the handoffs document abstract/catalogue-level evidence for the accepted records, not full-text appraisal. IEEE Xplore full-text access failed in the research portals. Mendeley is therefore treated only as a discovery and metadata index. Reported methods and results below are claims exposed by the accessible records; they are not independent full-text validation, study-quality appraisal, or replication. Confidence is capped at moderate for that reason.

- Normalized title/venue deduplication for DOI-less and all remaining records: no repeated normalized title or title/venue pair.

- Coverage among accepted records: AI 88; Cybersecurity 85; RAG 50; KMS 89. Tags overlap.
- Excluded after deduplication: C18, C19, C20, C22, and C23. Each was only metadata/search-snippet level in the handoff, without an accessible abstract; retaining them would make method or relevance assessment depend too heavily on metadata inference. Their exclusion is a quality screen, not a duplicate removal.
- Stop condition: satisfied under 01-plan-2 §5 because at least 100 unique, metadata-verified, thesis-relevant candidates remain with coverage across all four scopes. The synthesis therefore stops without another search pass.

## Unsupported claims

These are claims the thesis must not make from this evidence base. Unsupported-claim count: **5**.

| ID | Unsupported claim | Why unsupported | What evidence would be needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| U01 | The proposed system improves live SOC incident outcomes across organizations. | No accepted record full-text-validates this exact KMS in a live multi-organization SOC comparison. | Prospective or realistic field evaluation with representative analysts, incidents, baseline workflows, error severity, and operational outcomes. |
| U02 | RAG or GraphRAG is generally superior, cost-effective, or the best architecture for cybersecurity knowledge recovery. | Results vary by task/model; costs and equal-budget ablations are largely absent. | Controlled same-corpus comparison against search-only, no-RAG LLM, fine-tuned/prompted SLM, vector RAG, and GraphRAG with quality, safety, latency, and cost. |
| U03 | Ontology/KG grounding guarantees correct, complete, current, or non-poisoned knowledge. | The accepted evidence explicitly identifies incompleteness, quality problems, temporal change, and graph/corpus poisoning. | Longitudinal provenance and quality audit with adversarial graph edits, stale facts, conflicting sources, and measured detection/recovery. |
| U04 | The system is privacy-preserving, secure, legally compliant, or safe because it is local, federated, access-controlled, or retrieval-based. | The records identify leakage, membership inference, prompt injection, database injection, and poisoning; no complete control or jurisdictional assessment is validated. | System-specific threat model, penetration/privacy testing, access-control verification, data-protection impact assessment, and applicable legal review. |
| U05 | The system can safely automate incident response, scanning, remediation, code execution, or offensive actions without expert approval. | Capability evidence is dual-use and error-prone; benchmarks do not establish safe autonomous operation. | Predefined scope, sandboxed trials, safety case, human factors testing, failure containment, audit/rollback, and governance approval. |
